## Codex Tailoring Rules

- Extract software requirements from the job posting and match *exact product names* where supported by the user's experience.
- Evaluate individual tools, not just their category's importance score.
- Prefer documented hands-on tool usage to generic familiarity or a past request to obtain a software license.
- Do not infer proficiency in an entire product family from experience with a related product.
- Do not turn an ongoing learning effort into an established work qualification.
- Deduplicate repeated languages/tools (e.g., Python, MATLAB, ParaView) in the final resume.
- Distinguish products, programming languages, custom-built software, and methods/workflows.
- Preserve truthful names, abbreviations, and spelling from the source, but normalize layout.
- Do not modify the master inventory during tailoring.
