## Global Summary Rules

- Write a concise professional summary of **approximately 45–65 words** (usually 2–3 sentences), unless a job-specific instruction requires otherwise.
- Mention the Ph.D. in Mechanical Engineering when relevant. Emphasize *demonstrated work*, not generic adjectives.
- Prefer evidence from recent professional experience (Rheem, L&T) plus relevant UTSA research; other projects require a clear match.
- Do not claim a fixed number of years of professional experience unless dates and the type of experience counted are explicitly checked. Research and industry experience may overlap.
- Preserve completed versus *in development* status. Patent disclosures are not granted patents.
- Do not claim mastery of software, production deployment, licenses, certifications, or experience unsupported by the source libraries.
- Retain quantifiable achievements only with accurate baselines and context. Avoid packing multiple unrelated metrics into a single summary.
- Use job-description keywords naturally, without keyword stuffing. A template is a starting point, not a statement that must be used verbatim.
- Do not mention personal or immigration details in a professional summary.

## Summary Selection Instructions for Codex

1. Identify the job's primary technical requirements and choose the closest primary summary (`SUM-001`–`SUM-005`).
2. Optionally blend evidence from **one** secondary summary when the role spans disciplines.
3. Re-read the cited experience entries before finalizing any claim. References are navigation aids, not proof by themselves.
4. Do **not** automatically include every referenced accomplishment. Select 2–4 highly relevant capabilities or evidence points.
5. Ensure the summary is consistent with the skills, employer entries, and demonstrations actually selected for the finished résumé.
6. Avoid unsupported claims such as a fixed years-of-experience total, commercial production deployment, granted patents, or tool proficiency that is unverified.
7. Output a single 45–65 word summary by default, without a heading such as 'SUM-001' or metadata in the résumé.
8. Track the selected summary ID(s) and supporting accomplishment IDs in `resume_content.json` for auditability, but omit them from the final rendered résumé.
